Project / Neighbourhood lunch loop

Entry assessment / promising with a narrow wedge

Convenience is crowded. Collective ordering is not.

Existing tools optimize delivery or loyalty. Few help small teams coordinate repeat pickup from neighbourhood cafés. That creates a focused behavioral wedge without building a logistics network.

Best customerTeams of 5–20
Proof behavior2 orders / week
Biggest riskHabit formation

First-version scope / four-week validation

One ordering loop. No delivery, rewards, or marketplace.

Prove that a team coordinator can start an order, collect five choices, and submit one café pickup twice in the same week.

The route
