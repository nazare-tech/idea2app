Adjectives

Quietly direct. Anti-hype. Specific. Constructively critical.

Rules
Short sentences, plain verbs.
Say "first version" more than "MVP".
No em dashes: commas, colons, semicolons, periods, parentheses.
Lead with the next action.
Never promise instant startups or effortless success.
Words we never use
unlock, supercharge, revolutionize, 10x, powered by AI, seamless, effortless, game-changing, elevate, curated, premium, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world"

A tagline test: if it could appear on any other tool's homepage without anyone noticing, it is not finished.

How the register shifts
Marketing
Makes a claim and defends it. Shortest sentences in the system. This is the only place the brand argues with the category.
Product
Names the artifact and the next decision. No persuasion: the user already committed. Warm, factual, never chatty.
Errors and empty states
Says what happened, then what to do. Two sentences maximum. Never blames the user, never apologises at length.
Long-form output
PRDs, competitive reads, tech specs. Same craft as the marketing copy: in this product the artifact is the deliverable.
