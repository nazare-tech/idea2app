Channel rules
One claim per post
No threads unless asked. No hashtags, no emoji, no rocket. If the post needs a hook to work, the claim is not strong enough.
Photography is rationed
At most one photo per grid row. A photo used as a filler tile is the fastest way to look like every other tool.
Red marks the ask
Exactly one red element per composition, and it is always the thing you want clicked. Never a decorative red tile.
Motion
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 550ms for entrances, 200ms for state. No bounce, no spring, no parallax.
