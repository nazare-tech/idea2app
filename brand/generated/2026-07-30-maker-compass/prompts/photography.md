# Task starter — Maker Compass photography

Load `system-prompt.md` first, then this file. Works with gpt-image-2, Codex image
generation, Seedream, or any image model.

## The visual territory

A lit workshop, not a dark command center. Someone is in the middle of thinking a build
through on paper. The room has been used. The light is real light.

## Master prompt template

> Photographic, documentary, found-not-staged. [SUBJECT AND ACTION].
> [ROOM AND CONTEXT — real furniture, papers, plants, wear].
> Light: [window light / overcast daylight / low late-afternoon rake], never studio strobes.
> Palette: warm neutrals — warm paper, oatmeal, wood, off-white, warm grey, warm black —
> with exactly ONE red accent object in frame and nothing else red.
> Visible film grain, shallow-to-medium depth of field, editorial composition, slight
> imperfections. Subject is not looking at the camera.
> No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no handwriting that resolves into readable
> words, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no signage.

## Do include

- Paper artifacts: loose sheets with hand-drawn rectangular wireframe boxes and arrows in
  black ink, index cards in columns, pinned cards on a wall, kraft folders, a wooden ruler.
- Exactly one red object: a red felt marker, red tape, a red stamp mark, red thread.
- Rooms with a life in them — worn chairs, plants, a mug, stacked books, uneven stacks.
- Closed laptops as often as open ones. The brand is about thinking before generating.

## Never include in the prompt

studio strobes, softbox, "professional", "premium", "sleek", "modern office", coworking
space, glass partitions, "engineers at laptops", "team collaborating", "startup vibes",
"tech background", "innovation", neon, holograms, dark mission-control rooms, "clean white
background", floating objects, gradient backdrop, lens flare, bokeh lights.

## Banned cliché concepts

lightbulbs, hourglasses, handshakes, network nodes, glowing brains, rockets, ladders,
chess pieces, mazes, puzzle pieces, upward arrows made of glowing lines, robots, android
hands, humanoid AI figures, literal compasses held up to the camera.

## Substitutes for "a person doing X"

If a human is not required, shoot the evidence of the human instead: the table mid-thought,
the wall of cards with one card half-pinned, the notebook left open, the chair pushed back.

## Cast

Any image with people must be racially diverse across the set. Name a specific ethnicity in
each prompt rather than relying on the model's default, which skews white. Rotate across
Black, South Asian, East Asian, mixed-race, Latina, and Middle Eastern subjects, and vary
body types and ages, not only skin tone. If only one person appears, make that choice
deliberately.

## Screens in frame

Screens show plain light grey placeholder rectangles and at most one solid red rectangle.
No readable interface text, no charts, no icons. Any brand text is composited in later so
it stays editable and correctly typeset.

## Never name a real publication, brand, or person

Describe the visual quality you want instead. Naming a magazine is the fastest way to get
baked-in text and a moderation refusal.
