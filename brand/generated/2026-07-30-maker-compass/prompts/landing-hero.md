# Task starter — landing page hero copy

Load `system-prompt.md` first, then this file.

## Structure to return

1. **Kicker** — Fira Mono UPPERCASE, 2 to 4 words, `0.18em` tracking. Names the category or
   the moment, not a benefit.
2. **Headline** — 4 to 9 words. Display weight 800 at `-0.05em`. Must carry a claim.
3. **Subheadline** — 1 to 2 sentences, max 28 words. Names the actual outputs.
4. **Primary CTA** — 2 to 4 words, action-first, Action Red button.
5. **Secondary CTA** — quieter, ghost or outline. Reassurance or proof, never a second ask.

## Rules

- The headline must not be a feature list and must not contain "AI".
- The subheadline is where specifics live: market context, product plan, first-version scope,
  mockups, technical direction, next step.
- Do not stack two competing asks. One commitment per screen.
- No em dashes.
