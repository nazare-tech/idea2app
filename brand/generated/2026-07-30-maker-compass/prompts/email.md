# Task starter — marketing or transactional email

Load `system-prompt.md` first, then this file.

## Subject lines

- 6 words max. Lowercase-leaning sentence case. No emoji, no "Re:" tricks.
- Say the thing that happened or the thing to decide. Never tease.
- Good: "your first version scope is ready" / "one decision left on this idea"
- Bad: "🎉 Big news inside!" / "You won't believe what we built"

## Body

- Open on the reason for the email in the first sentence. No greeting flourish.
- Max 120 words for transactional, 200 for marketing.
- One link, one ask. The ask is the last line before the sign-off.
- Name the artifact by what it is ("your first-version scope", "the competitive read"),
  not by feature name.
- Transactional tone stays warm but factual. No exclamation marks.

## Sign-off

"— the Maker Compass team" or no sign-off at all for transactional. Never "Happy building!".
