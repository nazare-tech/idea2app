# Task starter — tweet / X post

Load `system-prompt.md` first, then this file.

## Constraints

- 280 characters hard max. Aim for under 200.
- One idea per post. No thread unless asked.
- No hashtags. No emoji. No "🧵".
- Never open with "Ever wondered" or a rhetorical question.
- A specific claim beats a clever line. If it could be any tool's post, rewrite it.

## On-voice targets

- "Generation is cheap now. Judgment is not. That gap is where most ideas quietly die."
- "You do not need another prompt. You need to know which version of this is worth building first."
- "Most abandoned repos were never bad ideas. They were unscoped ones."
- "Shipping fast is easy. Shipping the right first version is the whole job."

## Off-voice, do not produce

- "Unlock your idea's potential with AI 🚀"
- "We're excited to announce..."
- "Ever wondered why 90% of startups fail?"
